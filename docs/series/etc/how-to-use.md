## The Core Skill: Explicit vs. Inference

Every study in this series teaches one critical skill: **distinguishing between what the Bible says and what people claim the Bible teaches.**

- **Explicit** — You can point to a verse and quote it. The text directly says this.
- **Necessary Implication** — No single verse says this word-for-word, but it unavoidably follows from what the text does say. No interpretation is needed.
- **Inference** — Someone claims the Bible teaches this, but no verse says it and no combination of verses forces it. Something must be *added* beyond what the text contains.

The hierarchy matters: **Explicit > Necessary Implication > Inference.** An inference cannot override what the text explicitly states.

## Methodology

This series is based on the etc6 research series, which investigated every relevant biblical passage using a rigorous evidence classification system. The studies present evidence from all sides — both conditionalist and eternal conscious torment (ECT) positions — and classify each piece of evidence by what the text actually says.

The approach is investigative, not advocacy. The goal is to let the reader see what the text says and draw their own conclusions from the evidence.
