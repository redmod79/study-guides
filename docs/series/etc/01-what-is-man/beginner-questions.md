# Study 1: What Is Man?

*Biblical Anthropology and Human Nature*

---

> **A Note on How to Study**
>
> Throughout this series, we will learn to distinguish between what the Bible **explicitly says** and what people **infer** from the Bible. These are not the same thing.
>
> - **Explicit:** You can point to a verse and quote it. The text directly says this.
> - **Inference:** Someone claims the Bible teaches something, but no single verse says it. They have drawn a conclusion — possibly a good one, possibly not — but the conclusion itself is not in the text.
>
> This distinction matters more than you might think. Many beliefs people hold about the Bible are inferences, not explicit statements. Learning to tell the difference is one of the most important Bible study skills you can develop.

---

## Section A: How Was Man Created?

1. Read **Genesis 2:7**. What two things did God use to create man? What was the result?

2. Did man *receive* a soul, or did man *become* a soul? What does the text say?

3. Read **Genesis 1:20-21, 24**. What does God call the animals? (Hint: look at the phrase "living creature" in Hebrew — it is *nephesh chayyah*, the same phrase used for man in Genesis 2:7.)

4. If animals and humans are both called *nephesh chayyah* ("living soul" / "living creature"), what makes humans different? Read **Genesis 1:26-27**.

---

## Section B: What Happens at Death?

5. Read **Ecclesiastes 12:7**. What happens to the body at death? What happens to the spirit?

6. Compare **Ecclesiastes 12:7** with **Genesis 2:7**. If creation was dust + breath = living soul, what is death?

7. Read **Psalm 146:4**. When a person's breath leaves, what happens to their thoughts?

8. Read **Ecclesiastes 9:5**. What do the dead know?

9. Read **Ecclesiastes 9:10**. What work, knowledge, or wisdom exists in the grave?

10. Read **Psalm 115:17** and **Psalm 6:5**. Do the dead praise God? Is there remembrance of God in death?

---

## Section C: Can a Soul Die?

11. Read **Ezekiel 18:4, 20**. What happens to the soul that sins?

12. Read **Matthew 10:28**. What is God able to do to "both soul and body"?

13. Read **Leviticus 21:11** and **Numbers 6:6**. The Hebrew here literally says *nephesh meth* — "dead soul." What does this tell you about whether a soul can die?

---

## Section D: Who Has Immortality?

14. Read **1 Timothy 6:16**. Who has immortality? Does the text say "God and humans" or "God only"?

15. Read **Romans 2:7**. Must immortality be sought, or do we already have it?

16. Read **1 Corinthians 15:53-54**. When do believers receive immortality — at death, or at the resurrection?

17. Read **Genesis 3:22-24**. Why was Adam barred from the tree of life? What would it have given him?

---

## Section E: What Does the Bible Call Death?

18. Read **John 11:11-14**. What did Jesus call Lazarus' death? Then what did He clarify?

19. Read **1 Thessalonians 4:13-14** and **Acts 7:60**. What term did Paul and Luke use for death?

20. Read **James 2:26**. What is the body without the spirit?
